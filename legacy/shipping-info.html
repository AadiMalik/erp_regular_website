Shipping Information

Delivery options, timeframes and everything you need to know about getting your order.

Standard Delivery

Arrives within 2–4 hours. Free on orders over $50, otherwise a flat $4.99 fee applies.

Express Delivery

Arrives within 60 minutes for $7.99 — perfect for last-minute essentials.

Scheduled Delivery

Pick a convenient time slot for $2.99 and we'll deliver exactly when you need it.

Delivery Areas

We currently deliver within the service radius of each Smart Mart branch. Use the store selector in the header to choose your nearest branch and see accurate delivery estimates for your area.

Order Processing

Orders are processed as soon as they're placed. You'll see live status updates — Processing, Shipped, Out for Delivery, and Delivered — from My Orders or via Track My Order.

Delivery Attempts

Our courier will attempt delivery to the address provided at checkout. If you're unavailable, we'll contact you using the phone number on file to arrange redelivery.

Packaging

Fresh and frozen items are packed in insulated bags with ice packs to maintain quality during transit. Fragile items are individually wrapped for protection.

Questions?

For delivery issues on an existing order, visit Track My Order or reach out via Contact Us.
