Cancellation Policy

Last updated: August 1, 2026

Plans change — here's how order cancellations work at Smart Mart.

1. When You Can Cancel

Orders can be cancelled free of charge while they're still in the Processing stage — before they've been picked and handed to a courier. Once an order moves to Shipped, it can no longer be cancelled.

2. How to Cancel

Go to My Orders, open the order you'd like to cancel, and select Cancel Order. The cancellation is applied immediately.

3. Refunds for Cancelled Orders

If payment was already captured (card, PayPal), a full refund is issued to your original payment method within 3–5 business days. Cash on Delivery orders simply require no payment.

4. After Shipping

If your order has already shipped and you no longer want it, you can request a return once it's delivered — see our Returns & Refund Policy for details.

5. Cancellations by Smart Mart

Occasionally we may need to cancel part or all of an order due to stock unavailability or a pricing error. In this case, you'll be notified and refunded in full for the affected items.
